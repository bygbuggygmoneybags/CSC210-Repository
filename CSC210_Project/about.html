Hello there! As you might have guessed, i'm G Rodriguez-Rubio. I am a Computer Science Major at Neumont University. I was born and raised in Thornton, Colorado. Thornton is a city 11 miles north of Denver, Colorado. I went to Thornton High School and spent most of my life in the Denver Metro area. I grew up immersed in Chicano culture, enjoying artists my grandfather introduced me to such as Santana, Tierra, and El Chicano. I also grew up with Chicano art, like graffiti, tattoos, and drawings. I also grew up enjoying music from other cultures that Chicanos typically enjoy that heavily influences their art, such as black art forms like Hip-Hop, Jazz, and Soul.

My whole life, I have been a glutton for knowledge. I was raised with the philosophy that life is endless learning, that the moment you stop learning is the moment you stop living. I always had books, educational channels, and internet access to satiate my curiosities. This always caused me to be alienated from my peers because I never had one focus. My ideas were always going in a million directions at all times, unable to focus on a singular pursuit, often jumping between looking into multiple things I was curious about at once. This included a particularly strong attachment to technology. Between exploring the world of modifying video games at 12, teaching myself basic Python at 15, or reading the code to my favorite websites at 9, it's easy to see that i've always been fascinated by the digital space and its inner workings.

I chose to go into Computer Science not only because it's a very lucrative financial opportunity, but because there's always a chance to learn something. The field is constantly evolving and has emerging niches and increasingly efficient ways to accomplish tasks. This fact, along with my fixation on knowledge, led me to feel like I struck gold. With Neumont's Computer Science program, I learn so much about so many different disciplines, keeping myself curious about so many different branching paths on an exponentially growing tree.
